## ABOUT

I'm a designer who builds. I care less about whether a screen looks pretty and more about whether the thing works for the person using it — which matters a lot when the product is AI and the user is a developer.

I'm the founding designer at Entelligence AI. We're building a production reliability engine for engineering teams: it reviews every PR against the team's own incident history, watches production live, and heals what breaks — so the same class of bug never ships twice. For my first year I was the only designer here, so I owned most of it end to end: the product experience, the brand, the website (the one live right now — hero, motion, all of it), the launch videos, the engineering Leaderboard, Entelligence Wrapped, and the decks the founders take into fundraising.

Before Entelligence I was the only designer at Composio (agentic-AI infrastructure, now backed by Lightspeed). I built the pitch deck behind their $25M Series A, plus the SWE-Kit and MCP launch sites, launch videos, and creatives — and redesigned and rebuilt the company website along the way. Basically everything that had to look sharp in front of developers and investors for a year.

I didn't start in design. Mechanical-engineering diploma → digital marketing → design → now designing and building for AI. Somewhere along the way I stopped waiting for engineering to build my ideas and started shipping them myself: landing pages in code, motion graphics frame by frame, and AI agents that do real work.

I care about AI products people actually want to use and keep coming back to. If that's what you're building, we'll probably get along.
